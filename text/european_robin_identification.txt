Species: European Robin
Scientific name: Erithacus rubecula
Category: Garden songbird
Typical habitat: woodland, gardens and hedges
Geographical range: Europe and nearby regions

Identification
A small round-bodied bird with a vivid orange-red face and breast, brown upperparts, and pale underparts.

Habitat and appearance
European Robin is associated with woodland, gardens and hedges. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs. For an automated search engine, this document provides written context that can be matched to a picture or a text query.
