Species: Common Blackbird
Scientific name: Turdus merula
Category: Garden songbird
Typical habitat: parks, woods, gardens and farmland
Geographical range: Europe and other introduced ranges

Identification
Adult males are largely black with a contrasting yellow-orange bill and eye-ring; females are mostly brown.

Habitat and appearance
Common Blackbird is associated with parks, woods, gardens and farmland. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs. For an automated search engine, this document provides written context that can be matched to a picture or a text query.
