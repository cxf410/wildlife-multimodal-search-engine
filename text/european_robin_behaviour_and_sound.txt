Species: European Robin (Erithacus rubecula)
Topic: Behaviour and vocalizations

Vocalizations and behaviour
Often seen near ground level searching for insects, worms and other invertebrates. Its clear, varied song is associated with territorial behaviour.

Multimodal relationship
This note should be retrievable for the recordings and videos tagged european_robin. It provides a language description of the species, its habitat, and the sounds or actions associated with it. A sound recording and a video are distinct data items, even if they show the same type of behaviour.
