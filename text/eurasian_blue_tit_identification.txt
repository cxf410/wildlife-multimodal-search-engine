Species: Eurasian Blue Tit
Scientific name: Cyanistes caeruleus
Category: Woodland songbird
Typical habitat: deciduous woodland, parks and gardens
Geographical range: Europe and western Asia

Identification
Small bird with a blue cap and wings, white cheeks, a dark eye stripe, a greenish back and yellow underparts.

Habitat and appearance
Eurasian Blue Tit is associated with deciduous woodland, parks and gardens. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs. For an automated search engine, this document provides written context that can be matched to a picture or a text query.
