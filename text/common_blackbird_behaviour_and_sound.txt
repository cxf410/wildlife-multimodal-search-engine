Species: Common Blackbird (Turdus merula)
Topic: Behaviour and vocalizations

Vocalizations and behaviour
Forages on the ground for earthworms and other small animals, as well as fruit. Its melodious, fluty singing is common around sunrise and in the evening.

Multimodal relationship
This note should be retrievable for the recordings and videos tagged common_blackbird. It provides a language description of the species, its habitat, and the sounds or actions associated with it. A sound recording and a video are distinct data items, even if they show the same type of behaviour.
