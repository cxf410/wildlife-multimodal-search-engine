Species: Common Cuckoo (Cuculus canorus)
Topic: Behaviour and vocalizations

Vocalizations and behaviour
Famous for brood parasitism: females lay eggs in the nests of other birds. Male calls often have the recognisable repeated two-note cuckoo sound.

Multimodal relationship
This note should be retrievable for the recordings and videos tagged common_cuckoo. It provides a language description of the species, its habitat, and the sounds or actions associated with it. A sound recording and a video are distinct data items, even if they show the same type of behaviour.
