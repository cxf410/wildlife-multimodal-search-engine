Species: Eurasian Wren
Scientific name: Troglodytes troglodytes
Category: Woodland songbird
Typical habitat: dense vegetation, shrubs and undergrowth
Geographical range: Europe and parts of Asia

Identification
A very small, compact brown bird with fine barring, a short neck, and a short tail that is frequently held upright.

Habitat and appearance
Eurasian Wren is associated with dense vegetation, shrubs and undergrowth. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs. For an automated search engine, this document provides written context that can be matched to a picture or a text query.
