Species: Eurasian Blue Tit (Cyanistes caeruleus)
Topic: Behaviour and vocalizations

Vocalizations and behaviour
An active forager, often hanging from thin branches to find insects and spiders. It produces high, ringing notes and short repeated sequences of calls and song.
