Species: Red-winged Blackbird
Scientific name: Agelaius phoeniceus
Category: Wetland songbird
Typical habitat: marshes, wetlands, grassland edges and fields
Geographical range: North and Central America

Identification
Adult males are black with conspicuous red shoulder patches edged in yellow; females are streaky brown.

Habitat and appearance
Red-winged Blackbird is associated with marshes, wetlands, grassland edges and fields. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs.
