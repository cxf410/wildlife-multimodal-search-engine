Species: Common Cuckoo
Scientific name: Cuculus canorus
Category: Migratory bird
Typical habitat: open woodland, farmland edges and reedbeds
Geographical range: Europe and Asia; winters in Africa

Identification
A slender grey bird with long pointed wings, a long tail, and a pale belly with dark horizontal barring.

Habitat and appearance
Common Cuckoo is associated with open woodland, farmland edges and reedbeds. Its appearance can help distinguish it from other birds in a collection of wildlife photographs. For an automated search engine, this document provides written context that can be matched to a picture or a text query.
