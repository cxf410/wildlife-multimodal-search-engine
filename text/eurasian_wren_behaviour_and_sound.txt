Species: Eurasian Wren (Troglodytes troglodytes)
Topic: Behaviour and vocalizations

Vocalizations and behaviour
Moves quickly through low, tangled vegetation to look for insects and other small invertebrates. Despite its size, it produces a surprisingly powerful, fast and complex song.
